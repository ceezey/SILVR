Controls that exist today (verify they are intact):
- Daily-bar causality: daily_bars in src/silvr/strategy/trend/signals.py emits a daily bar only once its last 1m candle has closed and raises on an incomplete day. The trend engine (src/silvr/backtest/trend_engine.py) appends a day's close only at the first minute of the next UTC day, so breakouts, channel-midpoint stops and the 90-day volatility use only days that closed before the decision minute. Entries fill at the next day's first 1m open, never at the signal close, and a stop raised at day d's close first applies to day d+1's first minute (tests/test_trend_engine.py::test_no_lookahead_entry_and_stop). FAIL any change that fills at the signal close, uses a day before it has closed, or lets a stop, breakout or volatility value see a later minute.
- Intraday stop fills: a minute whose low reaches the stop closes the slice at the stop, or at the minute's open when it opened below the stop (test_gap_fills_at_open), and a slice entered at a minute's open is checked against that same minute's low (test_stop_checked_on_entry_minute). FAIL any change that fills a gap at the stop price, skips the entry minute, or fills at a price inside the bar that was not knowable.
- Same-bar handling: if a strategy with a take-profit is added, a candle that touches both the stop loss and the take profit takes the stop (pessimistic). Any change that takes the target first is a FAIL.
- Chronological splits: the trend runner splits the 1m range 0.6 / 0.2 / 0.2 by minute index (TRAINING_FRACTION, VALIDATION_FRACTION and segment_bounds in src/silvr/backtest/trend_runner.py); each requested segment runs on its own engine with a fresh $15,000 account, risk engine and kill switch and no open positions; the report records parameters_tuned false. chronological_split in src/silvr/backtest/validation.py (0.6, 0.2, 0.2) must stay ordered and non-overlapping.
