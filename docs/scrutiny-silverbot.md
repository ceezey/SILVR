## 1. Verdict

**Question:** should the project continue toward live trading?

**Verdict: PROCEED WITH FIXES.**

The foundations are real and tested (strategy engine, risk engine, backtest engine, paper execution engine, config validation; 356 collected tests from 293 test functions in 27 files at `40ea0b1`, 2026-10-14) and live trading is hard-blocked at config load (`config/settings.py:105-108`), so continuing carries no capital risk today. Since `099085b` backtest mode runs from the command line on downloaded Binance kline files. Since `973cd82` paper and demo modes run on closed candles polled from the Binance Futures public klines endpoint and fill trades locally; no order is sent to any exchange, and the network path is verified only with mocked transport (no live network run has been made). No strategy performance evidence exists (the evidence reports are empty until the owner runs the backtest on real data), and several safety gates are weaker than the old documents claim. Live trading must not be enabled until the fixes below are closed and evidence is produced. No performance claim may be made for the strategy until a non-empty evidence report exists.

Required fixes before any step toward live trading (order of priority). The HIGH safety fixes batch 1 ([spec](superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-high-safety-fixes-design.md), merged to `dev` at `70ccaec`) closed G-2, G-4, G-8, G-10 and G-11 and partly closed G-5, G-6 and G-7. The G-3 entrypoint batch 2 ([spec](superpowers/specs/2026-10-09-g3-entrypoint-design.md), code complete on `dev` at `099085b`) closed the backtest part of G-3 and H-4 and partly closed G-9 and G-12. The PRD-F2 to PRD-F5 batch (2026-10-14, `78f8dca`, `cf8d41f`, `973cd82`, `40ea0b1`) added the paper and demo runtimes and real klines access and partly closed G-13, G-20, G-21 and G-32. The items below say what remains.

1. Produce real evidence: run a backtest with non-zero costs and an out-of-sample split, and a paper run; store non-empty reports. See G-1, G-12. Backtest costs now default to non-zero (G-11, closed), `python -m silver_bot --candles data/candles` writes the backtest and out-of-sample reports with walk-forward windows, a Monte Carlo summary and a pass or fail verdict ([runbook-backtest.md](runbook-backtest.md), `78f8dca`), and `python -m silver_bot` in paper mode writes the paper report (`973cd82`); the owner has run neither on real data.
2. Make readiness meaningful: empty or invalid evidence files are now rejected (G-2, closed). The live-ready state is still unreachable (H-3) and nothing consults readiness (D-10), not even the paper and demo runtime built in `973cd82`; both stay documented.
3. Give the bot a real runtime: mode dispatch for backtest, paper and demo, and a supervisor-safe exit behaviour. See G-3, G-13. Mode dispatch and the backtest runtime are built (`099085b`, `f6bc017`); paper and demo sessions on polled market data with simulated fills are built (`973cd82`). Readiness is not consulted at startup and the testnet order path waits on an owner decision (G-17).
4. Fix the shipped configuration: `.env.example` must load. Closed (G-4).
5. Unify risk limits: one source of truth for per-trade risk, daily loss and leverage, capped at the documented 0.5%. See G-5 (partial: the cap, `build_risk_config` and `build_risk_engine` exist, CLI backtests use the settings caps and the paper runtime calls `build_risk_engine` since `973cd82`; daily-loss and leverage bounds are checked in demo mode only).
6. Make the kill switch effective by default, with automatic triggers and persisted risk state. See G-6, G-7 (partial: fail-closed check, auto-trip, SQLite state, an in-memory armed switch without a path and paper position close exist, and the paper runtime builds the engine with them; reset tooling is open, and a restart with an open paper position leaves `open_positions` at 1).
7. Harden the ML filter: the filter must be built from settings and model artifacts need trust checks. See G-9, G-23. NaN output now rejects (G-8, closed); settings reach the filter in backtests and paper sessions (G-9 partial; no model shipped).
8. Tighten the testnet URL guard to an exact host match. Closed (G-10).
9. Replace Telegram's in-memory client with a real transport and test it. See G-15.
10. Close the owner-input items in BRD §8 ([brd-silverbot.md](brd-silverbot.md)) and the open unknowns. See G-29, G-30.

## 4. Assumption Stress-Test

| Assumption | What would falsify it | Current evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The Silver Bullet strategy has a statistical edge on the chosen instrument | A cost-inclusive out-of-sample backtest with expectancy at or below zero, or results that vanish under walk-forward or small parameter changes | None. The backtest, out-of-sample and paper reports are empty (G-1). The engine and the backtest CLI exist and are tested on synthetic data only; no edge is claimed. |
| The fee and slippage model is realistic | Paper or demo fills that cost more than the model, or results that flip sign when slippage and spread are raised | Fees match Binance-style maker 0.0002 and taker 0.0005 defaults (`backtest/models.py:32-33`), and backtest slippage and spread default to 5 and 3 bps since `59ed6d5` (G-11); those values are not checked against real fills. The paper runtime uses the backtest cost defaults since `973cd82`; none is checked against real fills. Funding is not modelled (G-20). |
| The 1-minute timeframe is appropriate | Edge that disappears after realistic latency and costs on 1m, or signals whose frequency makes fees dominate | The default `TIMEFRAME` is 1m (`config/settings.py:76`). No evidence either way (Open). Fee drag on a 1m scalping style is the main concern. |
| Testnet (demo) behaves like live | Different liquidity, spreads, fills, rate limits or order-rejection behaviour between testnet and the production venue | None. Demo mode reads testnet klines since `973cd82` but places no order (G-17); the testnet is generally a thin, unrepresentative book, so it can validate plumbing but not performance. |
| The ML filter adds value | Filtered trades show no better expectancy than unfiltered trades on a held-out set, or calibration is poor | None. No model is shipped (S-23); the filter is disabled by default and wired to settings in backtests and paper sessions (G-9). ML stays optional and can only reduce risk or reject. |

## 5. Feasibility & Scope

- **Scope:** personal use by the owner; one instrument by default (BTCUSDT); research, backtesting, paper and demo first. Live trading is `Planned, blocked` and out of scope for this release (PRD-F12).
- **Feasible now:** hardening what exists (G-4, G-8, G-10 and G-11 are closed; G-5 to G-7 are partial), producing evidence with the backtest CLI on Binance kline files downloaded by the owner and with a paper session (G-1, [runbook-backtest.md](runbook-backtest.md)), and writing the canonical docs.
- **Larger work:** a WebSocket stream (G-13), the testnet order path (G-17, owner decision), Telegram transport (G-15), sensitivity and multi-symbol validation (G-12). These are new code and need their own plans (Planned).
- **Dependencies on the owner:** historical data source and capital figures (G-30). No cost, schedule or headcount estimate is made; none is available (Open).
- **Out of scope for the 2026-10-08 docs pass:** all code, test, config and deploy changes. Code fixes since then are listed in the [index](index.md) change log.

## 6. Risk & Compliance Pre-flight

- **Trading risk:** leverage up to 3x is the documented cap (`src/silver_bot/risk/models.py:28`); the bot must never trade without a stop loss, average down or use martingale (AGENTS.md rules; stop-loss requirement is enforced by the risk engine gates). Details in RFC-002 (risk and kill switch) and RISK sections of SDD.
- **Compliance:** personal trading on the owner's own account; jurisdiction and tax treatment are Owner input required (G-30). Venue terms of use, API-key withdrawal restrictions and key handling are tracked in CLR §2.
- **AI/ML risk:** ML is a filter only; NaN handling (G-8, closed), pickle loading (G-23) and leakage (G-22) are tracked in AIA §2.
- **Security:** no secrets in the repository; `.env` is never committed; the readiness secret scan exists but its behaviour outside git is Open (U-20, G-29).
- **Operational:** the restart loop is fixed (H-4, `f6bc017`): systemd does not restart on exit 2. Since `973cd82` the `bot` service runs a paper session that builds its risk engine with `build_risk_engine` and SQLite storage; a market data failure exits 1 and is restarted. A restart with an open paper position needs the manual `risk_state` reset (G-7).
